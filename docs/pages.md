# Pages

Both pages are the data role's, under **Manage**, for the plugin's admins.
Neither shows a quote, a price, a rate or a print. Each is also a read tool
on the deployment's MCP.

## Connection (`read_connection`)

- **Key:** the mode, whether the token is set, how many securities are
  listed; **Access**, a token, never a password; and **Securities**, how many
  resolved, its `…` each security's description and record (or why none).
- **Limits and use:** the pace kept, the calls made and those rate-limited,
  the last call and error.
- **Stream:** it does not stream.

## Datasets (`read_datasets`)

- What was not recorded or what the lake refused, with how often, in a
  notice whose `…` lists each.
- **Served:** its three datasets, whether each is licensed, what it recorded
  and declined, and the benchmark curve's record on the marketplace's row.
- **Wants:** the wants it answered and keeps current; **Subjects**.
